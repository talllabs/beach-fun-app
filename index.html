Where do the conditions come from?

Tides use the closest NOAA prediction station to each beach, including local subordinate stations, with heights in feet above MLLW. Water temperature comes from the closest NOAA sensor that reported in the last 3 hours, with its location, distance and measurement time. If that sensor is more than 10 miles away it is labeled “Regional water temperature”. Swell and wind are Open-Meteo model forecasts. Their grid cells span several kilometres, so neighbouring beaches can share values, and offshore wave height is not a breaking-surf estimate. Offshore and onshore wind labels use each beach’s approximate facing direction. Every reading shows when it was measured, separately from when the app checked.

Where does water quality come from?

Current advisories and closures are posted by each county: OC Beach Info and SD Beach Info. The app doesn’t check them yet, so tap the link before swimming. A good grade never overrides a current advisory. The latest grade comes from the closest active Heal the Bay sampling station. If that station is more than half a mile away it is labeled as a nearby sample, and beyond 4 miles the app shows “No local result available”. Avoid swimming within 72 hours after rain.

How does the history work?

Each morning, starting the day this feature launched, a scheduled job records every beach’s water temperature and latest grade. Each month is kept as its own archive file and nothing is deleted. Open a beach’s History to see the graphs and the full table.

How do drive times work?

Without your location, drive times are rough estimates from Dana Point. Tap “Drive times from my location” and your browser asks permission. Your location is rounded to about 100 metres and sent only to the routing service (Mapbox with live traffic, or a no-traffic estimate if live traffic isn’t set up). It is never stored, and drive times refresh every 10 minutes. Tap any drive time to open live-traffic directions in Google Maps, or Apple Maps on iPhone.

A camera won’t start?

Try its refresh button. iPhone Low Power Mode may require you to tap Play. Some providers block embedding or take feeds offline; a source link is always available.
