Add Beach Cams to your iPhone

Open the published site in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Live cameras and fresh conditions need an internet connection.

Where do the conditions come from?

Free NOAA Tides & Currents data. Each beach lists its nearby prediction station. Water temperature is a regional La Jolla observation, not a measurement at each beach. Times use Pacific time. Conditions refresh every 10 minutes.

Where does water quality come from?

Each beach links to its Heal the Bay Beach Report Card page, which grades beaches A to F from bacteria testing. The app shows the latest dry-weather grade and test date from one nearby Heal the Bay sampling station, rechecked about once an hour. “No recent test” means the station wasn’t sampled lately or the grade couldn’t load. Tap Heal the Bay for full details, and avoid swimming within 72 hours after rain.

A camera won’t start?

Try its refresh button. iPhone Low Power Mode may require you to tap Play. Some providers block embedding or take feeds offline; a source link is always available.
